📖 The Deflaters Analytics Handbook
🪵 Pine Tax (Cumulative Bench Cost)
The total number of real points surrendered to your bench across the season. It calculates the difference between your team's Optimal Score and your Actual Score each week.
Example: You started an RB who scored 4.2 pts while leaving an RB with 18.2 on your bench. That is 14.0 pts added to your Pine Tax.
🧲 Opp Surges Faced & Streak (X st)
Tracks how many times an opponent significantly outperformed their projected ESPN score when facing you. The (X st) callout denotes their current consecutive streak of facing surging opponents.
Example: 6/14 wks (3 straight!) means your opponent beat their projection 6 times this year, and you are currently in a 3-week stretch where opponents are spiking against you.
🍀 Luck Delta (Δ)
Quantifies schedule luck by measuring the gap between your Actual Win % and your All-Play Win %.
+0.450 (Lucky): Escaping with wins despite bottom-half scoring.
-0.500 (Unlucky): Putting up top scores but getting buzzsawed by the #1 scorer.
🌐 All-Play Record
What your record would be if you played every other team in the league every single week. It strips away schedule bias to reveal your roster's true scoring caliber.
In a 12-team league, going 11–0 in All-Play means you put up the absolute highest score of the week.
🧠 Coaching Efficiency %
The percentage of maximum possible points you successfully started. Calculated as: (Actual Score ÷ Optimal Lineup Score) × 100.
A score of 100% means you started the mathematically best possible lineup out of everyone on your roster.
🛡️ Avg Opp PA (Matchup Gauntlet)
Average Points Against per game. Teams at the top of this list have faced the most brutal schedules in the league, regardless of their win-loss record.

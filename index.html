📖 The Deflaters Analytics Handbook
🫀 The Cardiac Index
Tracks a team's record in nail-biting games decided by 5.00 points or fewer. Highlights which managers thrive under pressure and which ones repeatedly melt down in crunch time.
🧠 Lineup Efficiency (%)
Measures how close a manager comes to capturing their maximum possible points each week by evaluating actual starters versus optimal roster configurations.
📐 Pythagorean Expected Wins
Calculates a team's true expected record based purely on total points scored (PF) versus points allowed (PA). Exposes whether a team is genuinely elite or just benefiting from a soft schedule.
🍀 Schedule Luck Δ
The difference between a team's actual win percentage and their All-Play win percentage (how they would have fared against every team every week). Positive delta means you caught lucky weekly matchups; negative means you faced buzzsaws.
💸 The Pine Tax
Total actual points left sitting on the bench instead of in optimal starting slots due to manager overthinking or misjudged start/sit decisions.
⚓ The Anchor Award
Weekly dishonor awarded to the lowest-scoring active starter in the entire league, dragging their team's score down like an iron weight.

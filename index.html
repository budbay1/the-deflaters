📖 The Deflaters Analytics Handbook
🪵 Pine Tax (Cumulative Bench Cost)
Total real points surrendered to your bench across the season. It calculates the difference between your team's Optimal Score and your Actual Score each week.
Example: Started an RB who scored 4.2 pts while leaving an RB with 18.2 on the bench. That is 14.0 pts added to your Pine Tax.
🧲 Opp Surges Faced & Streak (X st)
Tracks how many times an opponent significantly outperformed their projected ESPN score against you. The (X st) callout denotes their current consecutive streak of facing surging opponents.
Example: 6/14 wks (3 st!) means opponents beat projections 6 times, currently in a 3-week streak of opponent scoring explosions.
🍀 Luck Delta (Δ)
Quantifies schedule luck by measuring the gap between your Actual Win % and your All-Play Win %.
+0.450 (Lucky): Winning despite bottom-half scoring.
-0.500 (Unlucky): Top scoring neutralized by opponent gauntlet.
🌐 All-Play Record
What your record would be if you played every other team in the league every week. It strips away schedule luck to show pure roster strength.
Going 11–0 in All-Play means you posted the highest score in the league that week.
🧠 Coaching Efficiency %
Percentage of maximum possible points started: (Actual Score ÷ Optimal Lineup Score) × 100.
100% means you started the mathematically best possible lineup from your roster.
🛡️ Avg Opp PA (Matchup Gauntlet)
Average Points Against per game. Teams at the top face the toughest weekly scoring schedules in the league.

📖 The Deflaters Analytics Handbook
🫀 The Cardiac Index (Clutch vs. Cursed)
Tracks team record in tight, high-pressure games decided by 5.00 points or fewer. Identifies who has ice in their veins versus who is snakebitten by heartbreakers.
Example: A 3–0 record means you routinely win nail-biters; a 0–3 record means you've suffered 3 brutal losses by a single play.
📊 Scoring Volatility (σ StdDev)
Statistical standard deviation of your weekly scoring totals. Measures whether your lineup is predictable or chaotic.
Steady Floor (σ < 12): Scores almost the exact same points every week.
Boom/Bust (σ > 18): Alternates between 160-pt explosions and 75-pt duds.
📐 Pythagorean Expected Wins
Adapts Bill James's sports formula: (PF² ÷ [PF² + PA²]) × Games. Calculates what your win-loss record should be based solely on point differential.
Example: An 8–2 team with only 5.8 Pythagorean expected wins is living on borrowed time and schedule luck.
🔨 Extremes of War (Gavel & Coin Flip)
Tracks the absolute matchup boundaries of the season.
The Gavel: Largest blowout of the year (biggest margin of victory).
The Coin Flip: Closest game of the year (smallest margin of victory).
⚓ The Anchor Award (Lead Weight)
The anti-bounty award given each week to the single lowest-scoring active starter across the entire league (benched players excluded).
Example: Starting a kicker or defense that puts up -3.00 points, or a WR who posts a 1-catch, 4-yard dud (1.40 pts).
🪵 Pine Tax (Cumulative Bench Cost)
Total real points surrendered to your bench across the season. Calculates the difference between your team's Optimal Score and your Actual Score each week.
Example: Started an RB who scored 4.20 pts while leaving an RB with 18.20 on the bench. That is 14.00 pts added to your Pine Tax.
🧲 Opp Surges Faced & Streak (X st)
Tracks how many times an opponent significantly outperformed their projected ESPN score against you. The (X st) callout denotes their current consecutive streak of facing surging opponents.
Example: 6/14 wks (3 st!) means opponents beat projections 6 times, currently in a 3-week streak of opponent scoring explosions.
🍀 Luck Delta (Δ)
Quantifies schedule luck by measuring the gap between your Actual Win % and your All-Play Win %.
+0.450 (Lucky): Winning despite bottom-half scoring.
-0.500 (Unlucky): Top scoring neutralized by opponent gauntlet.
🌐 All-Play Record
What your record would be if you played every other team in the league every week. It strips away schedule luck to show pure roster strength.
Going 11–0 in All-Play means you posted the highest score in the league that week.
🧠 Coaching Efficiency %
Percentage of maximum possible points started: (Actual Score ÷ Optimal Lineup Score) × 100.
100% means you started the mathematically best possible lineup from your roster.
🛡️ Avg Opp PA (Matchup Gauntlet)
Average Points Against per game. Teams at the top face the toughest weekly scoring schedules in the league.
